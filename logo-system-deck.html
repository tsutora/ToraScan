Tora Suite · Brand concept
Personal utility apps · v0.4

The big T,
the small tool.

A personal suite of utility apps behind one black app-icon. A big white T is what you recognise — and tucked beside its stem, one small orange icon of the tool tells you which app you’re about to open.

1920 × 1080 · Plus Jakarta Sans 01 / 08
Principle 01

Show the tool.
Keep the T.

A monogram alone never told you what the app was for. So the tool goes into the picture — one small, simple orange icon tucked beside the stem of the T. Never above the bar, never bigger than the letter: you read the T first, the function second, both in one glance.

Tora Suite — Principle 02 / 08
Anatomy

Three layers, one order.

The mark is a stack of three layers. Two are frozen so the family stays intact; the middle one carries the app.

01App-tile — a black rounded square. The universal app-icon silhouette, stripped to one colour.
02Tool icon — one small, simple orange drawing of the tool, sitting in the open space beside the stem. Never above the bar, never overlapping the letter.
03The T — a big white letter, grid-built, always in front. Identical in every app of the suite.

Flat, one accent, near-square corners — the mark obeys the same law as the deck around it.

Tora Suite — Anatomy 03 / 08
The suite

Two tools, one T.

Same black tile, same white T, same position. Only the small orange icon beside the stem changes — so the suite always reads as one family. Two apps ship today, Scan and Money; the next one drops into the same mark.
