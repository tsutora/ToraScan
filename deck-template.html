Tora Basic Rule — Structure 03 / 07
Method

Structure first.
Decoration last.

The slide is a 12-column field with a fixed 72px margin. Content is placed on columns, separated by 1px hairlines, and never centered.

Two type sizes do most of the work: one statement at 700–800, one support line at 300–400. Anything else is data.

What stays out

Shadows, gradients, icon sets, and stock imagery are not available at any level of this system.
